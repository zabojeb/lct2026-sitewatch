## Decision

The dataset is technically clean enough for labeling, but **not training-ready**. The pipeline blocks
training until all 100 tasks have a completed annotation, including explicit empty annotations for
frames without target equipment, and at least one valid bounding box exists in the dataset.

No labels, predictions or metrics have been fabricated to make the baseline appear complete.
